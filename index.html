About Me

As an independent and practicing clinical psychologist and psychotherapist, I am deeply invested in bringing to the world a consciousness to living and breathing. By that I mean working with people to find the insight to meet life and then work their way through it in a healthy, nourishing manner. I had been practicing as a psychotherapist since 1995 and it had been an incredible journey of meeting clients who had been courageous to overcome their own challenges.
